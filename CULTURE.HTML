WELCOME TO MIAGAO

Our Culture, Our Pride

Miagao is a municipality in Iloilo, Philippines, known for its historical landmarks, religious traditions, local customs, and strong community spirit.

Its heritage reflects the stories, beliefs, and creativity passed down through generations. These traditions continue to shape the identity of the Miagaowanons.

HISTORICAL LANDMARK

Miagao Church

Also known as the Santo Tomas de Villanueva Parish Church, Miagao Church was completed in 1797. It is famous for its unique Baroque architecture and detailed stone carvings.

The church is part of the UNESCO World Heritage Site known as the Baroque Churches of the Philippines.

Local Food

Explore Filipino and Ilonggo dishes offered by local eateries. Traditional food brings families together and helps preserve local food culture.

Traditions and Celebrations

Religious activities, festivals, and community gatherings help preserve the customs and traditions of Miagao across generations.

Arts and Local Crafts

Traditional craftsmanship represents the skills and creativity of local communities. Supporting local artisans helps keep these traditions alive.

Why Culture Matters

Protecting cultural heritage keeps the history and identity of Miagao alive for future generations.

  • Preserves local history and traditions.
  • Strengthens community identity and pride.
  • Encourages cultural tourism.
  • Supports local businesses and artisans.
